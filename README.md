The funniest toaster error message: one deadpan line a kitchen toaster shows its owner when something goes wrong. The bread has hired a lawyer, and the toaster — out of its depth, still on the job — can only reroute the crumbs to counsel. No toast pun, no setup that needs a second sentence: the surprise comes from the law, not the kitchen. A make.fun entry, judged pairwise by an AI judge on funniness, freshness and tightness.

The entry line (73 characters, one line):

> ERROR: The bread has hired a lawyer. Please direct all crumbs to counsel.
